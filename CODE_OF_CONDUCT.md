# Code of Conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate regardless of background, identity or experience.
Be respectful, give constructive technical feedback and respect other people's privacy.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions and restrict participation when needed.
This policy applies to this project's issues, pull requests and community spaces.
Report concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options on
https://github.com/tranvanhuy-hichan. Do not publish private details in a public report.
